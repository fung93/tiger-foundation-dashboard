How UNI trade accounting works

History counts confirmed UNI swaps and 1inch limit-order fills on Base and BNB Chain, including fills submitted by a resolver. Average buy price is weighted across recorded purchases. Sale results use the carried strategy cost, including tokens returned from LPs. LP deposits, withdrawals and claims are not buys or sells.

Trade prices use the tokens actually paid or received in that transaction. Supported dollar stablecoins use the stated $1 anchor. Unverified swaps or missing dollar costs keep cost-based results unavailable. UNI held counts actual wallet UNI and UNI inside open tracked LPs. Capital is fresh funding minus withdrawals, including funds used to buy UNI or to fund the stablecoin side of a UNI LP. Reinvesting returned funds does not add capital. Value now includes wallet UNI, open LPs, returned strategy cash and pending principal or fees, counted once. P&L equals Value now minus net Capital, split between settled results and current gains or losses. An LP reaching its upper edge is still open; settled LP results require confirmed withdrawal and collection. Settled values keep their original transaction prices, and the existing LP Book history stays unchanged. Native network fees are excluded from P&L. Source blocks and transaction links remain attached to the saved history.
