How UNI trade accounting works

The swap book counts confirmed wallet-initiated UNI buys and sells on Base and BNB Chain. Each network keeps its own average cost; a sale removes the sold UNI at that cost. LP operations and claims are not buys or sells. Wallet balances and LP inventory are shown separately and are not added to the trade-book value.

Trade prices use the tokens actually paid or received in that transaction. Supported dollar stablecoins use the stated $1 anchor. Unverified swaps or missing dollar costs keep cost-based results unavailable. Network fees are listed in their native token and are excluded from P&L. Source blocks and transaction links remain attached to the saved history.
