How UNI trade accounting works

The swap book counts confirmed UNI swaps and 1inch limit-order fills on Base and BNB Chain, including fills submitted by a resolver. Each network keeps its own average cost; a sale removes the sold UNI at that cost. LP operations and claims are not buys or sells. Wallet balances and LP inventory are shown separately and are not added to the trade-book value.

Trade prices use the tokens actually paid or received in that transaction. Supported dollar stablecoins use the stated $1 anchor. Unverified swaps or missing dollar costs keep cost-based results unavailable. UNI held counts actual wallet UNI plus UNI principal and unclaimed owner UNI fees in open tracked LPs. Value now includes wallet UNI and both tokens in open UNI LPs, including unclaimed owner fees. Capital is the actual dollars paid for recorded UNI buys. P&L equals Value now minus Capital. This compares current tracked holdings with gross purchase spending; sale proceeds and earlier distributions outside those holdings are not added. Historical LP results keep their recorded prices in LP Book. Owner-paid network fees are listed in their native token and are excluded from P&L. Source blocks and transaction links remain attached to the saved history.
