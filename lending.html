How these limits are calculated

Your minimum health is your personal setting. At health 2, your debt limit is half the market’s liquidation limit. Your maximum LTV is the market’s LLTV divided by your chosen health.

The charts use collateral valued by each market’s Morpho oracle, including at historical blocks. The separately shown market value uses avKAT’s redemption rate and KAT’s pool price. These values can differ.

Average LTV is total debt divided by total oracle collateral across the markets. Empty markets do not dilute the percentage. An inactive market has no collateral, lending or borrowing. avKAT/USDC is hidden while inactive and returns if a position opens again.

Debt includes interest accrued to the reading’s block. Borrowing room is also limited by the loan tokens currently available in that market. Each market stands on its own; room in one does not offset a repayment in another.

Choose Edit to change a target. Save applies it to that market’s graph and amounts, including the limit across recorded history. Unsaved changes leave the graph at your saved target. Saving does not change the recorded collateral or debt. Each opening or reload starts at 70% max LTV for avKAT/KAT and 35% for both USDC markets. Saved edits apply only for this visit.

The KAT liquidation prices for USDC loans are estimates from the current oracle relationship. They assume debt, collateral amounts, avKAT conversion and other oracle inputs stay unchanged. The actual trigger is Morpho’s oracle and accrued debt. For avKAT/KAT, both sides are in KAT, so there is no single KAT dollar price that triggers liquidation; its avKAT-to-KAT ratio is shown instead.

Each market’s chart section has its own All, 7D and 14D controls for both charts. Each chart starts with its first borrowing; All shows the whole loan history. The 7D and 14D buttons zoom into recent readings. Older history is sampled daily and recent history every six hours, rather than recording every price move. Times are shown in Malaysia time. Missing readings appear as gaps.
