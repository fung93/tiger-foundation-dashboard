How these numbers are made

Trading fees. The six pairs are KAT/USDC, USDT/USDC, avKAT/KAT, avKAT/USDC, vbETH/USDC and vbWBTC/USDC. Funded Sushi V3 fee tiers appear separately. KAT LP emissions are excluded.

Volume and APR. Volume counts each swap's input once over the last 24 hours, valued at this reading's token prices. LP fees apply the observed protocol fee split. Fee APR annualises those fees over the pool's current TVL; it is not a forecast.

Your daily estimate. Your capital and range determine liquidity. The estimate uses the last seven days of fee growth and your conditional share of current active liquidity. The price must stay in range; future volume and competition can change. Totals and hedges use a seven-day comparison period.

Hedge. BTC and ETH exposure use their corresponding Katana Perps markets. avKAT exposure can use KAT at its observed conversion ratio, which leaves avKAT/KAT ratio risk. Live venue prices, funding and costs are required; an unavailable hedge quote does not become a free hedge.

Sources. Verified Sushi pools and archive state on Katana, complete swap logs, and Katana Perps. This page reads only and never connects a wallet.
