    The pool

    Uniswap v3 USDG/CRCL 0.30%, Robinhood Chain: 0x654e4143e82a5824445ade0824351c2a9acd95a8. On 18 Sep it held $2.10M and traded $22.8M in 7 days. Live price, liquidity and volume are read straight from this pool.

    The perp

    Hyperliquid xyz:CRCL (trade.xyz builder market). Isolated margin only, max 10x, so maintenance is 5%. Settles in USDC, so USDG has to be swapped to USDC first. Order size can be typed in USD on Hyperliquid, which is why this desk speaks in USDC.

    Funding is paid hourly. The 30-day average is refreshed every 30 minutes. On 18 Sep it was +10.1% a year and positive 86% of hours: shorts were getting paid.

    How the numbers are made

    Fees = your share of active liquidity × 7-day average volume × fee tier × 5/6, counted only while in range. This pool's protocol fee is switched on and keeps 1/6 of every swap fee. The perp is assumed to track the pool at today's gap. Hedging cost = σ²·L·√P / 4 a day: the loss a continuously re-hedged LP realises, which is the impermanent loss that hedging cannot remove.

    Volatility: 30 days of hourly perp candles, refreshed every 30 minutes (4.7% a day on 18 Sep). Largest 7-day moves in the 120 days to 18 Sep: −30% and +28%.
