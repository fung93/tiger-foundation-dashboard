How these numbers are made

What counts. Every swap of KAT for a stablecoin (vbUSDC, vbUSDT) by either wallet on Katana since the buy on 27 Oct 2026 at 16:02. That covers a swap in one transaction (a router or a filled limit order) and the swap service that takes a deposit and pays back in a later transaction. Borrowing or repaying KAT on Morpho, avKAT deposits, LP rewards and LPs are not trades and stay off this page; the LP Book and Lending pages carry them.

The book. Average cost. A buy adds its KAT and what it cost. A sale takes its KAT out at the average cost at that moment; the difference from what it fetched is realised. The average buy price is the cost of the KAT still held over that KAT. Unrealised is what that KAT is worth at today's price less its cost. If a sale were ever bigger than the KAT this book holds, the extra came from outside it (rewards or borrowing) and is left out of the P&L and marked in the table.

Prices and fees. Each trade is priced by what actually left and reached the wallet, so the route's fees are already inside it; the fees column shows them anyway: the service's and relayer's cut, and the gas the wallet paid at the ETH price when the trade was recorded. KAT today is the Sushi KAT/vbUSDC pool's price; the chart values the KAT held at each hour's closing price in that pool, and at each trade's own price.

Trade simulator. Runs the trades you type after the real ones, on the same average-cost book: a buy spends the dollars at the price given, a sale sells the KAT at that price and realises the difference from the average cost then. Everything after is valued at today's price. Enter the price you would actually get: the routes' fees are inside the price, as they are for the real trades. The break-even price is the KAT price at which the total P&L, realised and unrealised, would be zero. Nothing is saved or sent.

Sources. The wallets' token transfers and the pool's swaps, read from Katana. A six-hourly job keeps the ledger; anything newer is read when the page asks. This page reads only; it never connects a wallet.
