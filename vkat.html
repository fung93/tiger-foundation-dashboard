How these numbers are made

vKAT. The KAT locked in the vKAT the two wallets hold now, read from Katana's voting escrow; its voting power is the same. KAT in avKAT is not counted here: the avKAT vault votes it and compounds its rewards itself.

Votes. Every vote and reset the gauge voter recorded for either wallet. A vote stands from epoch to epoch until it is changed, so an epoch counts as voted when a vote stood during it. Its pool and vKAT are the vote that stood with the most vKAT behind it in that epoch (the latest, if equal): a lock moved into avKAT after the vote week does not hide the vote it cast.

Rewards. The vote incentives Merkl paid each vKAT lock, named by pool and lock (AragonBribes). Each belongs to the epoch its campaign ends in, which is that epoch's vote week, and is valued at its token's price when the campaign ended; vbUSDC is a dollar. Rewards are booked once and never re-valued.

APY. An epoch's reward over the vKAT that voted, valued at KAT's price when that epoch's vote week closed, compounded over the 26 epochs of a year as if each reward were put back in; the simple rate (APR) is shown beside it. The average is all the rewards over all the vKAT voted, for the epochs whose vote week has closed. Leaving vKAT takes a 60-day cooldown and a fee of 2.5% to 25%, which these rates do not include.

Sources. Katana's gauge voter and voting escrow, Merkl's reward records and campaigns, and the KAT, vbETH and vbWBTC pools for prices at each moment. A six-hourly job keeps the ledger; anything newer is read when the page asks. This page reads only; it never connects a wallet.
