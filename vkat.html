How these holdings are counted

vKAT. KAT locked in the vKAT NFTs currently held by your two tracked wallets.

avKAT. Wallet balances plus your avKAT collateral in Morpho, valued at the live KAT conversion rate. This is the gross token holding; borrowing is shown separately on Lending. avKAT inside LPs is accounted for on LP Book.

Katana network. The network row keeps the escrow's total locked KAT, total avKAT supply and the live KAT-per-avKAT conversion rate. avKAT backing is already included in network vKAT.
